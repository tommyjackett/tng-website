EP 139

The Rise of Adrian Portelli and LMCT+

The boys open a new season and unpack Adrian Portelli, the 34-year-old who bought three of five houses at The Block finale. Jason walks through Portelli's back story, from the McLaren craned into his apartment and the dummy-bidder press to LMCT Plus, the online rewards club that sidesteps gambling rules by bundling discounts from 750 partner brands with free giveaway entries. They dig into the numbers behind a reported 70 million dollar turnover and a billion-dollar paper valuation, the social proof from real winners, the FOMO it drives, and the Costco-style subscription model. They finish on how the model is a legal trade promotion, comparing it to Macca's Monopoly, and celebrate the entrepreneurial mindset behind it.
