EP 159

The Rise and Fall of F45

Jason unpacks the rise and fall of F45, the Aussie fitness franchise that scaled to 2,200 studios across 60 countries, IPO'd at a 1.4 billion dollar valuation with Mark Wahlberg backing it, and has since collapsed to around 114 million with over a hundred studios for sale. Using a smartcompany.com.au teardown, he shows how percentage-of-revenue franchise fees punished growth, how branded-equipment margins and pulled financing hurt franchisees, and how the numbers and brand value unravelled. Nick and Marty then zoom out to franchising in general: easily duplicated fitness fads, handcuff exit terms, doing due diligence on the franchisor's own financials, and whether you're better building your own brand than paying half your revenue for a name on the door.
