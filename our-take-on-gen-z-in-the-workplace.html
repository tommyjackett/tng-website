EP 182

Our Take On Gen Z in the Workplace

Marty brings a reflection to the table: as a Gen Xer raised to tow the line and never talk about mental health, he has been rethinking how he leads the Gen Z employees now coming through. The trio unpack what is different about the generation born 1997 to 2012, how they crave autonomy inside clear principles, openly discuss mental health in appraisals, prefer phone or Zoom over face to face, and embrace technology as natives. Nick pushes back on entitlement, argues you cannot argue with results, and warns not to lose the bottom line chasing inclusive or environmental initiatives that his own Gen Z workforce ignored. Jason ties it to the accounting profession struggling to attract Gen Z because of its boring image. They land on adapt or die: foster the innovation, empower younger staff, offer mentorship and flexibility, and keep learning across every generation.
