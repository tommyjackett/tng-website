Driving Business Success with Passion and Belief

Welcome to Episode 128 of The Numbers Game. In this episode, we explore the foundations of business success—passion, belief, and the execution of a good idea. Marty shares the story of his son Charlie's coin business, and how it reignites essential questions about why we do what we do in business. We also discuss the recent acquisitions of Zimmerman and WeWork to delve into the drivers behind successful and not-so-successful businesses.
