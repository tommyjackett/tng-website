EP 149

Don't Fail to Scale

Unpacking the Challenges of Business Growth

Marty leads the trio through why so many businesses stall at $1 to $1.5 million in revenue and what it really takes to scale to the next level without losing what made them successful. They cover strategizing from where you want to be, setting stretch goals, standing on the shoulders of giants who have walked the path, hiring excellent salespeople, setting performance standards above the industry average, not burning cash on speculation, doubling down on what works, and simplifying to scale. Jason adds the accountant's view on forecasting and reading your numbers, and Nick shares his own experience of plateauing and seeking outside advice.
