EP 157

Super Bowl Ticket Madness

Nick unpacks the eye-watering cost of getting to the 2024 Las Vegas Super Bowl, where the average ticket ran about 12,000 US and a box cost 1.2 million, and asks why American sport runs on pure supply and demand while Australia does not. The trio compare the US secondary market to AFL scalping, dynamic pricing at the Australian Open, and legitimate resale sites like StubHub, and weigh whether fans should be able to legally resell their tickets. Nick explains the NFL's 32 Equity fund and its On Location ticket allocation, Jason draws a parallel to buying Xero shares early, and Marty tells how he once flipped an 8,000 dollar corporate box into free grand final seats. They close torn on whether demand-based pricing shuts out loyal fans.
