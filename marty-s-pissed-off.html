EP 165

Marty's Pissed Off!

Marty is fired up about Victoria's ballooning state debt, and the trio turn a cost-of-living gripe into a full vent about government spending. It opens with a $97 breakfast and a stung-pocket takeaway pizza, then Marty lays out the numbers: state debt rising from 55.2 billion in 2019 toward 247 billion, with roughly 40 million dollars a day and 14 to 15 billion a year going just to service interest. They walk through the revenue conundrum, the post-COVID exodus from Victoria, investors selling up and moving to Queensland, Perth and Adelaide, and a run of costly projects, the empty 1.1 billion prison near Geelong, the desal plant, the scrapped Commonwealth Games, the airport rail gap and the 125 billion suburban rail loop. They close on the fix they'd want, business leaders on an advisory board and real performance KPIs for politicians.
