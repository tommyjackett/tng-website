EP 152

Revised Stage 3 Tax Cuts Explained

Jason, the accountant on the show, walks Nick and Marty through the Albanese government's revised Stage 3 tax cuts. He explains what the original legislated cuts did (a flat 30 percent bracket up to 200 thousand that mostly benefited high earners), how the revision slashes the top-bracket benefit from around 9 thousand to 4,500 while lifting the 60 thousand earner from 375 to 1,179, and the new bracket structure with a 16 percent rate, a 30 percent band to 135 thousand and a 37 percent band extended to 190 thousand. The trio debate whether it is the right call given cost-of-living pressure, why it is not really a backflip, and Jason's case for broader tax reform and lifting GST. He also flags the hit to business owners' bucket-company and family-trust planning and busts the marginal-bracket myth that stops people taking a pay rise.
