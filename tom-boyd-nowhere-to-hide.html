Tom Boyd - Nowhere To Hide

Welcome to episode 69 of The Numbers Game. On today's show, we're joined by Tom Boyd. At the age of eighteen, Tom joined the GWS Giants as the AFL's number 1 draft pick and was later recruited to play for the Western Bulldogs on a record $7 million contract. In 2016 Tom was instrumental in the Bulldog's drought-breaking Premiership win. However, in 2019, following years of struggle with depression and anxiety, Tom made the decision to walk away from the AFL. Tom is now a public speaker and mental health advocate.
