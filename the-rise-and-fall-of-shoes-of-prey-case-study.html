The Rise and Fall of Shoes of Prey - Case Study

Welcome to episode 97 of The Numbers Game. In this episode, Marty shares a case study on the rise and fall of Shoes Of Prey. We discuss the importance of financial planning, the effects of decision fatigue, and brand building strategies.
