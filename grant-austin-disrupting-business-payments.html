EP 173

Grant Austin - Disrupting Business Payments

Jason and Nick host Grant Austin of pay.com.au, with Marty away, to unpack how he built a fast-growing B2B payments and rewards platform. Grant walks through his chartered accounting and mergers and acquisitions background, why he wanted to be operational rather than a consultant, and how pay.com.au was spun out after a friend acquired Point Hacks. He explains the product, the Switzerland of payments and rewards positioning, the working capital and points use cases for business owners, and why prioritisation is the key to managing fast growth. The conversation covers winning airline partnerships as a startup with no track record, raising capital from family offices while avoiding venture capital, the weight of shareholder responsibility, when to launch a product, the ATO tax treatment of reward points, using AI in marketing, and exit planning.
