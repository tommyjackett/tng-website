The New Australian Dream

Welcome to Episode 113 of The Numbers Game. In today's show, we explore a report on Australians' shifting financial priorities. We look at the rising trend of financial independence, its growing precedence over home ownership, and the lasting impacts of COVID-19 on Australians' financial preferences.
