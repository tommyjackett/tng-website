EP 151

Maximising Productivity in 2024

Back for 2024, Jason, Nick and Marty run through the productivity hacks of nine global and Australian CEOs, plus the ones they use themselves. They cover meeting-free days, no-meeting mornings and 20-minute meetings, using commutes and fake commutes as thinking time, deadlines inside emails, reducing email volume and picking up the phone instead, block scheduling and the ditch-delay-delegate to-do list, shortening meetings to leave action gaps, tools like Decisions and Fireflies, and deleting social media apps to claw back an hour a day. Jason closes on his personal goal for the year: learning to say no before he hits burnout.
