EP 179

The Cost of Sitting on the Sidelines

Marty opens with SQM data on ten-year property growth against wage growth, showing capital cities up roughly 96 to 97 percent while wages barely moved, then walks through a cost-of-living squeeze that leaves two average incomes short of an average mortgage. The trio unpack the cost of sitting on the sidelines: why waiting for rate cuts or a crash is a losing bet, how the wealth gap and the baby boomer transfer of wealth widen the divide, and why getting in realistically matters. They land on practical strategy, rentvesting, boutique or regional options, and talking to professionals, backed by Jason's personal story of missing 2019 auctions before COVID pushed prices out of reach, and close with a mental health note.
