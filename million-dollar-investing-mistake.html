EP 164

Million Dollar Investing Mistake

Jason brings an article claiming Aussies make a million-dollar mistake by pumping every spare dollar into the mortgage instead of investing. The numbers: an extra $200 a week off the loan saves about $558,000 in interest over 30 years, but the same $200 in an index fund at a historic 9.8 percent grows to roughly $1.565 million. The trio then unpack why it is not that simple, the capital-gains-tax-free growth on your main residence versus tax on shares and dividends, why an index fund is really a diversified market you rarely need to sell, and how super's tax advantages and locked-up nature make it the priority once you have a 12-month cash buffer. They close with rapid-fire myth-busting and the reminder that there is no single right strategy.
