EP 303

The Inheritance Tax Hiding in Your Superannuation

Australia doesn't have an inheritance tax... yet. But look inside your superannuation and it tells a slightly different story. When super is passed on to adult kids, a big chunk of it can be taxed at 17%, and most families don't see it coming. Today Nick takes us through how that tax works and the strategy that can cut it, so more of what you've saved ends up with the people you're leaving it to.
