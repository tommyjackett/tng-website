EP 156

How to Win Friends and Influence People

Nick brings the ten leadership principles from Dale Carnegie's How to Win Friends and Influence People and the trio works through each one for business owners and leaders. They cover beginning with praise, calling out mistakes indirectly, owning your own mistakes first, asking rather than ordering, letting people save face, praising the slightest improvement, giving people a reputation to live up to, and making people happy to do what you suggest. Marty reframes the old shit sandwich, separates vulnerability from victimhood, and argues you earn the right to hard conversations by praising 80 to 90 percent of the time. Jason shares how a single praise email lifted a team member's whole day and how he elevates his team in front of clients. They close on healthy competition, leaderboards and non-monetary rewards like a reserved car park, agreeing it's often the recognition, not the prize, that drives people.
