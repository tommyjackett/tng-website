The Price of Corporate Greed

Welcome to episode 120 of The Numbers Game. On today's show, we look into the broader impact of corporate strategies on Australia's cost of living. We examine the pricing tactics of major corporations such as Coles, Woolworths, and Qantas, and consider how these may interact with inflationary trends.
