Christmas Shutdown Costs and the $300 Party Rule

The office might go quiet over Christmas, but the costs don't. Wages and super keep going out through the break, which is why the holidays can catch small business owners out. Jase runs the numbers on a 10-person business closing for three weeks, and it adds up to around $60,000. For businesses that aren't trading over the break, that can be brutal, and it doesn't stop there. Shutdown notice rules, payday super deadlines and fringe benefits tax on the Christmas party are all worth getting right. Today Jase takes us through what you need to be across, so you can plan ahead and head into the break with no surprises.
