Increasing our Natural Efficiency

Welcome to Episode 177 of The Numbers Game. Jase is back from running the London marathon and shares his observations on how the UK is tackling renewable energy vs where Australia is on the green-scale. We explore Australia's heavy reliance on mining and fossil fuels, the country's infrastructure for supporting sustainable habits, and the path forward for Australia in the green energy arena. A slightly different ep from our usual programming but certainly a topic worthy of the conversation.
