EP 143

The Future of Working from Home

Nick reopens the working-from-home debate as big employers push people back into the office. He flags Origin Energy and Suncorp tying office attendance to bonuses, and a Herbert Smith Freehill survey where 37 percent of executives plan to differentiate pay between remote and in-office staff. The trio weigh culture versus results, whether office workers should be paid more to cover travel costs, and how graduates miss mentoring at home. Nick then connects it to offshoring and foreign investment, citing wealthy Indian families investing into Australian businesses and roles being outsourced at 10 to 15 percent of the cost. The takeaway for employees: be visible, contribute to culture, add value and make yourself hard to replace.
