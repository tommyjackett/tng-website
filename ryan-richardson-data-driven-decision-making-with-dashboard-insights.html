EP 145

Ryan Richardson - Data-driven Decision Making with Dashboard Insights

Jason, Nick and Marty welcome the show's first sponsor, Ryan Richardson, an accountant, partner at T&M Consulting and founder of Dashboard Insights and Synced. Ryan explains how being a slave to a time-based business drove him to build Dashboard Insights, a data analytics product that connects to a business's data to surface the operational activity that drives financial outcomes. He shares his recurring what's-the-worst-that-can-happen mindset, how he empowers his team with authority and control points to avoid being the bottleneck, and his journey into offshoring through Synced after three attempts. The conversation covers innovation, the practical use of AI in professional services and transport logistics, a recycling client that went ten times on profit, and his belief that focusing on people first solves most business problems.
