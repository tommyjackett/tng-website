EP 162

The Business of Formula One

With the Melbourne Grand Prix approaching, Jason unpacks the numbers behind the business of Formula One. He digs into the 1994 Australian Grand Prix Act and the roughly 100 million dollars of state government money that keeps the event here, the F1's own books running at a 35 million dollar loss, and the flow-on economic benefit to Victoria through hotels, travellers and spending. The trio then trace how Netflix's Drive to Survive transformed the sport, doubling revenue, lifting the company valuation to around 17 billion and pulling in a young, more female audience. They finish on the eye-watering money in the sport, from Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton's contracts to the financial backers who decide who gets a seat.
