Growing Business Through Quality Relationships

Marty opens a conversation on how quality business and personal relationships drive growth, arguing you can't buy a relationship, you can only invest in one. Jason recounts meeting Nick over coffee at Dead Man Espresso through a mutual friend, a connection built on genuine value with no expectation of a referral. The trio unpack Marty's four questions for sizing up a referral partner, why values alignment beats chasing activity, and how to make time to meet new people. Nick shares the accountability piece that changed their referral partnerships, setting documented targets on lead flow and revisiting them quarterly, and they close on finding complementary businesses across any industry.
