Lessons from a 1300+ Day Running Streak

Jason and Marty, with Nick away, welcome back Greg Bramich, Jason's business partner at Future Advisory, two years after his first appearance. Greg is now 1,306 days into a daily running streak that began with a lockdown bet against Jason and has evolved into training for the six global major marathons. He talks through the couch-to-marathon transformation, the habits and prep that keep the streak alive, the day a virus nearly ended it, and why recovery now matters more than running. The conversation draws lessons on consistency, mindset, support networks, and the 'why not' attitude, and connects them back to leadership, mental strength and being a better business advisor.
