EP 168

Behind The Billions - Australia's Rich List

The trio go behind the billions of the AFR Rich 250, unpacking how a billionaire's net worth is actually calculated. Jason explains that net worth is assets minus liabilities, not cash, why ASX-listed companies are easy to value while private companies, illiquid assets and swinging Bitcoin prices create paper billionaires. They walk through new faces on the list, from Gina Rinehart and Nicola Forrest to Chemist Warehouse, Tim Heath's online-gambling YOLO group, data centres and crypto, then debate the mindset of the ultra-wealthy, tall poppy syndrome, and whether billionaires owe anything back through jobs, super, tax and philanthropy.
