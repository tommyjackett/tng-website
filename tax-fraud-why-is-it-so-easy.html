EP 160

Tax Fraud - Why Is It So Easy?

Sparked by an AFR report of a suspected 180 million dollar tax fraud, Nick asks Jason how these schemes work and how they slip past the ATO for years. Jason unpacks phoenixing and labour-hire structures that dodge PAYG withholding, the math of how the money disappears, and why under-resourcing lets it run. He shares a client who never lodged tax for 18 years without a single overdue letter, the 4.6 billion dollar TikTok GST refund fraud, and the Bank of Samoa super scheme. The trio weigh whether AI and tougher penalties can fix a self-assessment system built on trust, and Jason explains new safeguards like director IDs and agent linking.
