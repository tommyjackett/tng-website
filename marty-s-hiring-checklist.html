EP 170

Marty's Hiring Checklist

After a run of bonus money hacks - refinancing off 7 percent mortgages, parking spare cash in a high-interest account, and checking your super fund's investment options and fees - Marty walks through the hiring checklist he built to put his 25 years of intuition on a page. He grades candidates out of 130 points on things like job-hopping, whether they blame others, loyalty, proactivity and business fit, and finds clear outliers who score 90-plus. The trio then cover why the wrong hire costs you opportunity, why you should overpay slightly for quality, onboarding and the 30/60/90 day interview, using probation properly, the 'trend is your friend' rule, and how and when to let someone go.
