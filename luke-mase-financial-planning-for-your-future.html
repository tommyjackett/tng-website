EP 175

Luke Mase - Financial Planning for your Future

Marty and Nick host Innov8 senior financial planner Luke Mase, with Jason away in Europe, for a ground-level look at what a financial planner actually does. Luke busts the myth that you need to be rich to get advice, explains how getting your super risk profile and asset allocation wrong can cost tens of thousands over time, and walks through the power of compounding. The bulk of the chat covers retirement planning, the four types of personal insurance and how to fund them through super, estate planning, and the mechanics of moving from accumulation into pension phase. Luke shares real client cases, including a trauma claim payout, and shows how projections and Centrelink strategies can add years to a retirement.
