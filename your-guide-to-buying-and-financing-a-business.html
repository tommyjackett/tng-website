Your Guide to Buying and Financing a Business

Welcome to Episode 191 of The Numbers Game! In this episode, we get into buying and financing businesses in Australia. We explore the fundamentals of acquiring a business, including the importance of due diligence, evaluating your readiness, financial planning, and the critical role of professional advice. Marty provides a detailed overview of the financing process, outlining the criteria lenders look for, and the difference between secured and unsecured loans. Whether you're considering buying a franchise or expanding your existing business through acquisitions, this episode goes through the whole process so you can make informed decisions.
