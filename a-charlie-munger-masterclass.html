EP 144

A Charlie Munger Masterclass

Marty brings nine Charlie Munger lessons to the table and the trio apply each one to business and everyday life. From it's the strong swimmers who drown and the danger of overconfidence, to facing change before it forces you, dropping the victim mentality, addressing problems early, being a little wiser for a long time, and the patience of buy-and-hold investing where the big money is in the waiting. Marty runs the numbers on tax-deferred compounding, then closes on Munger's incentive principle with a live consulting story of an air conditioning firm that lifted output and revenue by giving the crew time back for quality work.
