EP 183

New Solution for Aspiring Home Owners Unlocked

Nick brings an AFR article to the table and unpacks Coposit, a platform that lets buyers secure a brand-new off-the-plan or house-and-land property with just $10,000 down and pay the rest of the deposit off, interest-free, while it is being built. The trio walk through why the deposit is the biggest barrier to buying, how the normal 10 percent deposit sits idle for up to four years, and how Coposit connects buyers and developers to get deals done quicker. Nick then explains the developer side, how CBA now accepts a Coposit deposit as a presale to free up construction funding and help housing supply, and how a signed contract supercharges buyers' savings behaviour. They close on the flat Melbourne apartment market, Brisbane's growth, and CBA's Property Share product for co-buying with friends or family.
