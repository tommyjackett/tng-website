EP 155

The Digital Heartbeat of Business

Jason leads the boys through how deeply businesses actually use the technology they pay for. Nick explains Innovate's big tech stack across four revenue streams, tech you must use versus tech of choice, and how HubSpot turned assumptive data into real-time insight. Jason works through Xero being underused: bank rules to auto-code recurring transactions, automated transfers, custom reports, invoice reminders, credit card payments, budget versus actual, and Hubdoc for receipts, plus why good records keep an ATO review from becoming an audit. They cover AI in business (Podium review replies, chatbots, loan assessment, Microsoft Copilot) and then the human side of adoption: showing the why, burning the boats, building a tech-savvy culture, appointing a tech champion, and not being over-stacked.
